The problem

Doctors don't need more software. They need less friction.

The prescription sits at the centre of the consultation. When it is slow or disconnected, everyone feels it.

01 — DOCTOR

Too much repetitive work

Repeated medicine searches, dosage entry and prescription formatting consume valuable consultation time.

02 — PATIENT

Instructions get lost

Paper prescriptions can be misplaced or misunderstood, especially when patients need simple, accessible instructions.

03 — FOLLOW-UP

The care journey breaks

A prescription should not be the end of the interaction. Digital delivery creates a bridge to follow-up care.
