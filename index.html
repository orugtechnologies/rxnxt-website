The gap after prescribing

Writing the prescription is not the end of treatment.

The real challenge begins when the patient leaves the clinic. Medicines have to be remembered, taken correctly and followed through.

01

Patients forget

Busy routines make it easy to miss doses, especially when treatment involves multiple medicines or timings.

02

Treatment gets interrupted

Without timely reminders and follow-up, patients may stop or delay treatment before the prescribed course is complete.

03

Doctors lose visibility

After the consultation, it can be difficult to know whether patients are staying on the treatment plan.
