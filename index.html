The Core Problem

One Paper Prescription. Three Breakdown Points.

A prescription is not just paper — it's a vital contract between three parties.

🩺

Doctor's Time Drain

Doctors spend 3 to 5 minutes writing repetitive paper prescriptions manually, leading to consultation backlogs and fatigue.

🧑

Patient Confusion

Patients misplace paper scripts, forget dosage schedules, and miss crucial follow-up visits — hurting their health outcomes.

💊

Pharmacist Risk

Pharmacists waste time guessing unreadable doctor handwriting, creating dangerous risks of dispensing the wrong drug.
