Frequently Asked Questions for Doctors

Everything you need to know about implementing RxNXT in your practice.

Will RxNXT slow down my high-volume OPD? ▼

No. RxNXT is designed specifically for high-speed Indian outpatient departments. With typo-tolerant fuzzy search and 1-click dosage chips (`1-0-1`, `After Meals`), most doctors generate a complete prescription in under 20 seconds—which is faster than writing by hand.

Do my patients need to download any separate app? ▼

Zero app downloads required. Patients receive the crystal-clear official PDF prescription and automated daily medication reminders directly on their existing WhatsApp.

Is the prescription legally valid under Indian Medical guidelines? ▼

Yes. RxNXT is fully compliant with National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations, displaying the doctor's registration number, qualifications, digital signature, and patient DPDP Act 2023 consent.

Can I customize my clinic's branding and drug preferences? ▼

Yes. You can upload your clinic logo, header letterheads, digital signature, and import your own favorite medicine brands via CSV. The search engine automatically prioritizes your clinic's favorite brands at the very top.

How long does setup take? ▼

Under 5 minutes. Since RxNXT is cloud-native, there is zero hardware installation. Simply open any web browser or tablet and start prescribing immediately.
