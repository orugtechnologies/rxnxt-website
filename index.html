Frequently Asked Questions

Everything doctors and clinic administrators ask before starting.

Will RxNXT slow down my high-volume OPD? ▼

No. Speed is the #1 KPI of RxNXT. With typo-tolerant additive search, shorthand aliases (`PCM` $\to$ Paracetamol), and 1-click dosage chips (`1-0-1`, `After Food`), most doctors complete a prescription in under 20 seconds—which is faster than writing with a ballpoint pen.

Do my patients have to download an application? ▼

Zero app downloads. Everything reaches your patient directly on their existing WhatsApp chat: the formatted A4 PDF prescription, morning dose reminders (8 AM), afternoon dose reminders (1:30 PM), night alerts (8:30 PM), and Day-25 chronic refill alerts.

Is my clinic data safe and isolated from other doctors? ▼

Yes. RxNXT is built on PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) hosted on Supabase Mumbai AWS (ap-south-1). Every clinic is mathematically isolated. Your patient contacts, diagnoses, and prescription habits cannot be viewed or shared with any other clinic or third party.

Is the digital prescription legally valid in India? ▼

Yes. RxNXT adheres to National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine and Prescription Guidelines. Every prescription includes the doctor's verified NMC/State Council registration number, qualification, clinic contact details, and digital signature.

What happens during the 14-Day Free Trial? ▼

You get full unrestricted access to RxNXT for your clinic. You can test prescription creation, WhatsApp PDF dispatch, and token queues with live patients. No credit card is required. Our clinical team assists with initial setup in under 5 minutes.
