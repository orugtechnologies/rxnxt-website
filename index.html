The gap after the consultation

The doctor finishes the consultation. The patient's treatment journey has just begun.

A prescription captures the doctor's plan. But what happens after the patient leaves the clinic can determine whether that plan becomes treatment in real life.

The prescription is clear. Life is not.

Patients return to busy routines, work, family responsibilities and everyday distractions. Even when they intend to follow treatment, the plan can become difficult to keep up with.

→Are doses being taken at the right time?
→Will the patient remember the full course?
